MATH BLASTER

Shoot the correct answer bubbles!

HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective: Read the question in the bottom box and shoot the falling bubble with the correct answer.

❤️ Lives: You have 3 lives. Missing the correct answer or shooting a wrong bubble costs a life.

📈 Progression: Clear 5 correct answers per level across 5 levels (25 total) to win!

⌨️ Controls:

  • Keyboard: A/D or Left/Right arrows to move, Space to fire.
  • Mouse: Move to aim, click to fire.
  • Touch: Drag to aim, tap the red FIRE button.
