How to play

  • MoveArrow keys or WASD. On a phone or with a mouse, use the on-screen pad.
  • BombPress Space or the bomb button. It explodes in a cross shape after two seconds.
  • ScoreBrick +10, monster +100, level cleared +200.
  • Power-upsB extra bomb, R longer blast, S more speed. Bricks sometimes drop them.
  • WinDefeat every monster to clear a level. Clear all 3 levels to win.
  • LoseYou have 3 lives. Stay out of blasts, including your own, and away from monsters.
